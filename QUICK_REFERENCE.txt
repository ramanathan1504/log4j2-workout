🔍 WHAT THE FIX DOES

  Problem:
    • At midnight, 1000s of machines compress logs simultaneously
    • Causes massive disk IO spike → threatens cloud stability

  Solution:
    • New attribute: maxCompressionDelaySeconds="N"
    • Adds 0-N seconds random delay before compression
    • Disperses compression events over time
    • Reduces IO concentration at scale

  Config Example:
    <DefaultRolloverStrategy max="20" maxCompressionDelaySeconds="3"/>

  Impact:
    ✓ Backward compatible (default=0, no delay)
    ✓ Configurable per user needs
    ✓ Automatically distributes compression events
